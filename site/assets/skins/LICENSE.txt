Original Astrax classic and slim pixel textures

classic.png and slim.png are original 64x64 pixel clothing/face designs made by
an explicit drawing script (tools/default-skins.py), not copied Mojang Steve or
Alex textures. Steve and Alex refer here only to standard model proportions.
No third-party texture inputs or AI image/model generator is used.
